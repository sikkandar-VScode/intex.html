About Fixlit

Quick help

How do I report an issue?

Choose New report at the top, or press n. Add a title, location and priority. The report is routed to the right team automatically.

How do I track an issue?

Open an issue with the ⋯ button to see its status, assigned team, due date and activity log. You can also change the status straight from the list.

What does Overdue mean?

An issue is overdue when it is still open after its due date: 1 day for High priority, 3 days for Medium and 7 days for Low.

How do I find an issue quickly?

Press / to search by title, ID or location. Use the status, priority and sort menus, or click a summary card or category bar to filter.

When should I call the officials?

Call for anything urgent or unsafe, like water leaks, broken locks or electrical faults. For routine problems, a report is enough.
